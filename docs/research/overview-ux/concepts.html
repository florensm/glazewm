GlazeWM overview · research

Five ways the overview could feel

Each plays on its own. Click a demo to take over: click a window to pick it, the background to cancel, or a closed desktop to open it. Each lists what it would take in GlazeWM: an option that exists, a new option, or new code.

01 · RECOMMENDED DEFAULT

Settle

The overview as it is, tuned: windows end at 0.8× instead of 0.69×, fill their slab, and land with a hint of give. The others dim a little as you point.

  • codeCluster cap 0.94 (expose.rs), margins 32 (layout.rs), chrome from 60% open.
  • optionwallpaper.padding: 16, expose.gap: 24, effects.stagger_ms: 10, window_lift: 0.04, dim: 0.12.
  • new optionSeparate open and close easings: open spring(0.85, 520), close spring(1, 800).
02 · SEARCH FIRST

Spotlight

Typing already searches. Here the matches also take the room: they gather in the middle, growing as the query narrows, and the rest bow out to a shelf along the bottom instead of fading in place. Enter takes the first.

  • existsThe exposé already spreads only the matches; non-matches fade to a quarter.
  • codeNon-matches to a shelf of small previews (native/scenes.rs spread_windows), still clickable.
  • codeSearch box grows from the field rather than appearing, 180 ms.
←→Enter
03 · WORKSPACES FIRST

Ribbon

For moving between workspaces rather than windows: every workspace in a row at the same size, the selected one in the middle, its neighbours peeking in on both sides as GNOME's do. Arrows slide the ribbon; Enter zooms in.

  • existsThe stack layout, turned on its side; the activities' slide between workspaces.
  • new optionstack.direction: horizontal, neighbours at 0.86× and 60% opacity.
  • codeWheel and touchpad glide the ribbon continuously, settling on the nearest, as the stack does today.
04 · PLAYFUL

Bloom

Opened from a hot corner, the windows peel off nearest the cursor first and overshoot a touch, like cards dealt from that corner. Picking one sends a ripple out from it. All motion, no extra waiting: the last window starts within 200 ms.

  • optionhot_corner.corner: bottom_left, effects.ripples: true, stagger_ms: 26, animation.bounce.
  • codeStagger outward from the cursor rather than the focused window (ripple_delays origin).
  • new optionOpen easing spring(0.62, 380): an 8% overshoot.
AltW→↓
05 · QUICKEST

Hold to pick

A tap of Alt+W opens the overview as today. Holding Alt makes it work like Alt+Tab: arrows move the ring, letting go of Alt picks. Switching window becomes one gesture of the hand.

  • existsArrows move spatially; Enter picks.
  • codeTrack the keybinding's modifier while open; its release picks once a selection was moved (wm.rs keyboard hook).
  • new optionoverview.release_picks: true.
